# Character designs (pilot cast)

Text design sheets. Each gives the silhouette, costume, palette and acting notes that the AI paint-over and the 3D proxies both follow. Colors and energy codes come from `02-style-bible.md` section 3. All faces are original; never prompt for a real actor's likeness.

Proportions: heroes are 8.5 heads tall (larger than life); Bhishma 9 heads; Bhima 8 heads but 1.5× shoulder width. The silhouette lineup is `art/silhouette-lineup.png`.

## Krishna (Kesava, Madhava, Hrishikesa)

- **Role in pilot:** charioteer, then the cosmic form.
- **Silhouette:** slim, relaxed contrapposto; a single peacock feather rising from the crown gives a unique top shape; flowing yellow upper cloth that trails in wind.
- **Face:** calm, almond eyes, faint smile, never strained. Ganguli notes "curved eye-lashes" (line 8673).
- **Costume:** yellow silk dhoti and upper cloth; gold crown (low and simple on the field, towering on the universal form); necklaces, armlets (Angada), earrings (line 8672); Kaustubha-style chest jewel as a single bright point.
- **Props:** reins; conch Panchajanya (white, gold-tipped); Sudarshana chakra appears only as FX.
- **Universal form:** "diadem, mace, and discus, a mass of energy, glowing on all sides" (line 4860), with faces and eyes on every side (lines 4843–4858).
- **Acting:** stillness is power. He moves least in every shot; the camera is locked when he speaks.

## Arjuna (Partha, Dhananjaya)

- **Role:** the archer who doubts, then rises.
- **Silhouette:** tall, narrow, upright like an arrow; long bow Gandiva makes a strong curve against his straight line.
- **Face:** young, sharp brows, curly hair ("O thou of curly hair", line 4830).
- **Costume:** white and silver scale armor on the torso, blue sash, wrist guards, a simple crown band.
- **Props:** Gandiva (larger than him when strung, dark horn and gold); two quivers; conch Devadatta.
- **Acting:** tight, coiled; the Gandiva drop is the biggest gesture in the pilot.

## Bhishma (the grandsire)

- **Role:** opens the battle with a lion's roar and his conch (lines 3475–3478).
- **Silhouette:** very tall and straight, a pillar; long white beard and hair; his tall palmyra-and-five-stars standard behind him (lines 2760, 6631).
- **Costume:** white and silver armor, white dhoti; plain, no gold except a band on the brow and the gold palmyra on his standard.
- **Acting:** heavy dignity; one large movement only (the roar and the conch).

## Duryodhana

- **Role:** Kaurava prince, watching Bhishma with fierce joy.
- **Silhouette:** broad shoulders, heavy wedge shape; mace on his shoulder.
- **Costume:** crimson and black, heavy gold ornament, serpent motif on the standard.
- **Acting:** restless, leans forward, grips the rail.

## Bhima (Vrikodara)

- **Role:** blows the huge conch Paundra (line 3482).
- **Silhouette:** wide boulder mass, thick neck, mace.
- **Costume:** saffron and iron, minimal upper armor; bare arms.
- **Acting:** explosive; his conch blast gets the second-largest FX ring.

## Yudhishthira

- **Role:** the eldest; blows Anantavijaya (line 3483).
- **Silhouette:** upright, square, still; a parasol behind him signals kingship.
- **Costume:** cream and royal blue; modest crown.
- **Acting:** controlled; the camera treats him symmetrically.

## Crowd and secondary

- Nakula and Sahadeva, Drona, Dhrishtadyumna, Sikhandin, Virata, Satyaki, Drupada, the sons of Draupadi, Abhimanyu: mid-ground figures with readable standards, not hero detail.
- Armies: three silhouette tiers (foreground detailed, mid painted, background flat shapes).

## Design deliverables per character (next step)

1. Front, side and back turnaround (AI paint-over of the 3D proxy, then cleaned).
2. Expression sheet (6 faces).
3. Color callout with the hex codes.
4. A character LoRA trained on 20–40 approved turnaround images, so every shot stays on model.
