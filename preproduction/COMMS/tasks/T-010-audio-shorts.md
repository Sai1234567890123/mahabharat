## Goal
Create the audio for the YouTube Shorts test cut, including a music bed and sound effects timed to the 84-second animatic.

## Inputs
- `art/motion/PICKS.json`
- `art/motion/animatic_v01.mp4`

## Steps
1. Build a music bed and sound effects (conch, chariot, impacts, the Vishvarupa swell), timed precisely to the shot durations in `PICKS.json` and the 84-second animatic.
2. Source only royalty-free or original music/SFX for which we have a clear license.
3. Record the source and license information for all audio assets in a new file `COMMS/audio_licenses.md`.
4. Export the final audio mix as `art/shorts/audio_v01.wav`.
5. Create a timing sheet (`art/shorts/audio_timing.md`) detailing the cues and duration markers.

## Done when
`art/shorts/audio_v01.wav`, `art/shorts/audio_timing.md`, and `COMMS/audio_licenses.md` exist.

## Do not
Do not mix the audio into the video. Do not upload anything. Do not use any unlicensed music or sound effects.
