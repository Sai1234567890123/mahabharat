## Goal
A tracking sheet for the reach test in `shorts/PLAN.md`, so each Short's numbers are logged the same way.

## Steps
1. Write `shorts/REACH.md`: a table with one row per Short. Columns: title, upload date and time, views at 1 hour, 24 hours and 7 days, average view duration, viewed-to-swiped (Shorts feed), subscribers gained, notes.
2. Add a decision block: what counts as a good result, and what would make us change the caption, the hook or the length. Mark the thresholds as our own rules, not platform data.
3. Leave all numbers blank. Sai fills them in after each upload.

## Done when
`shorts/REACH.md` exists with the table and the decision block. Set the task to `review`.

## Do not
Invent any numbers or claim typical reach figures.
