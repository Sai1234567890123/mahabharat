# YouTube Shorts Upload Checklist

**Rule:** Nothing is posted until Sai explicitly approves the final cut. Do not upload directly from the repository.

## Pre-Upload Mix (in Video Editor)
1. Import the video master: `art/shorts/mahabharat_shorts_1080x1920_silent.mp4`
2. Import the audio stem: `art/shorts/audio_v01.wav`
3. Verify that the audio length perfectly matches the video master (exactly 97.0 seconds).
4. Verify the on-screen title card is present and looks correct.
5. Export the final muxed video.

## YouTube Upload
1. Upload the final muxed video as a YouTube Short.
2. Use the metadata provided in `art/shorts/shorts_metadata_v01.md`.
3. Set visibility to Sai's preference (Unlisted for preview, Public when approved).
4. Post.
