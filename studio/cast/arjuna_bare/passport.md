# @arjuna_bare · passport v3 · variant of @arjuna

Use from A14 to the end. Sai, 2026-11-16: "remove hair band of arjuna". He is bare-headed with loose hair and no cloth band. (Ganguli, Karna Parva B08 line 11743, has him bind his locks with a white cloth; this trailer leaves that out by sai's choice.)

## Descriptor (paste verbatim, never shorten)

Arjuna, same man and same face as the reference sheet, Indian warrior-prince and archer in his thirties, dark warm brown skin, thin upturned black moustache, NO crown and nothing on his head: bare head, loose black wavy hair to the nape, a few strands across the forehead, bare ears with no earrings, a plain forehead with no mark, dust and sweat on his face, the same sleeveless carved gold cuirass with black enamel inlay, one scorch mark across the right shoulder guard, wide gold bracers, deep green silk sash with gold embroidery over a white dhoti, the bow Gandiva with black limbs wrapped in spiral gold bands.

- **Drift check:** any crown, cloth band, earring, tilak or gold on the head means a failed shot. No shield.
