# Character Passport: Bharata (the boy)

**Role:** Son of Shakuntala and Dushmanta; future emperor who gives his name to Bharatavarsha.
**Appears in:** `s1e01_the_scribe` sc03 (V08–V18)

---

## Descriptor

Bharata at six years old, known first as Sarvadamana — the subduer of all. A compact, fearless boy with his mother's warm brown skin, his father's physical boldness, and something that is entirely his own: a complete absence of fear. He wears bark-cloth shorts and a simple cord at the waist, no shirt, bare feet. His hair is cut short and practical. His eyes are his most distinctive feature — large, dark, and utterly unimpressed by lions. He does not perform bravery; he simply does not register danger. When he stands before the court at the end he is not awed by it — he has ridden a lion; a court is nothing.
