## Karna on the 17th day

- **Earrings and natural armour gone.** He was born with the golden kavacha (breastplate) and kundala (earrings) of
  Surya, and his name means "the ear-ringed one". Before the war he cut both from his own body as a gift to Indra
  (disguised as a Brahmin), knowing the cost, and received a one-use spear in return. So on the 17th day: NO earrings, NO
  natural armour; his gold cuirass is ordinary war armour. This matches the locked look (bare earlobes). Optional, subtle
  and bloodless: faint healed scars at the earlobes for close-ups, if sai wants that story beat.
- **Bow:** Vijaya, gift of his guru Parashurama.
- **Charioteer:** Shalya, king of Madra, who agreed only under pressure and taunts Karna during the drive.
- **Chariot:** well equipped, many banners, rows of bells. Canon horses are WHITE; our passport uses dun horses on
  purpose so the two teams read apart on screen (keep, or switch to white if sai prefers canon).
- **Standard:** the elephant's rope (hastikakshya), likened in the text to Indra's bow (a rainbow) across the sky.
- **Curses in the duel:** Parashurama's curse makes him forget the Brahmastra mantra at the moment of need; a Brahmin's
  curse makes the earth swallow his chariot wheel. He steps down to free the wheel; Arjuna, urged by Krishna, kills him
  with the Anjalika (Anjalikastra), named after the joined-palms gesture.
- **Serpent arrow:** Karna's snake-arrow (Ashvasena, Takshaka's son, in later tellings) is aimed at Arjuna's neck; Krishna
  presses the chariot into the earth and it only strikes Arjuna's diadem off.

## Arjuna on the 17th day

- **Diadem:** Kiriti ("the crowned one"), the celestial diadem from Indra. It is the crown the serpent arrow knocks off,
  so a separate crown piece in the rig is useful.
- **Bow and quivers:** Gandiva, given by Varuna (Agni's reward for the Khandava fire) with twin inexhaustible quivers,
  a divine chariot and celestial horses.
- **Horses:** milky white (his name Shvetavahana, "he of the white horses"), on a white-gold chariot.
- **Charioteer:** Krishna. **Banner:** the ape banner (Kapidhvaja, Hanuman on the flagstaff; Bhagavad Gita 1.20).
  **Conch:** Devadatta (Gita 1.15); Krishna blows Panchajanya.
- **Complexion:** one of his names is "Krishna" (the dark one); our passport's dark warm brown skin fits.
- Face and clothing colours are not described in these sources; our passport (lean, sharp profile, thin upturned
  moustache, green sash over white dhoti) stays the design source.
