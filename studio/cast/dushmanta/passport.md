# Character Passport: Dushmanta

**Role:** King of the Puru dynasty; antagonist-turned-protagonist of the Shakuntala arc (S1E01 scenes 07–17).
**Appears in:** `s1e01_the_scribe` sc02 (H01–H10), sc03 (V04–V18)

---

## Descriptor

Dushmanta, king of the Puru line. A tall, broad-shouldered Vedic warrior king in his late thirties, long black hair tied back with a gold cord, clean-shaven jaw, strong brow. Wears a warrior's armour in the forest: plate chest guard of hammered copper over a cream cotton dhoti, copper arm-guards, a quiver of arrows on his back, no sword in the hermitage scenes. In court scenes: unadorned deep-red cotton with simple gold thread borders, no jewelled crown — a plain copper circlet. His face in the forest is open and struck by wonder; in the court denial scene it is deliberately closed and unreadable; in the final recognition it opens fully. Strictly Vedic era 1000 BCE: no stirrups, no saddle-horns, no machined metal.

## Physical Description

- **Age:** Late 30s

- **Marks:** None ritual; a hunter's callus on his right fingers
- **Forest costume:** Copper plate armour, cream cotton dhoti, quiver, bare feet in the hermitage
- **Court costume:** Deep red cotton with thin gold border, copper circlet, simple sandals
