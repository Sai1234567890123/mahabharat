# Character Passport: Kanwa

**Role:** Forest sage; adoptive father of Shakuntala.
**Appears in:** `s1e01_the_scribe` sc03 (V02–V07)

---

## Descriptor

Kanwa, the forest sage. An elderly man, late sixties or older, with the weathered look of a life lived outdoors in honest tapas — not gaunt or dramatic, but radiating quiet warmth. White hair worn in a simple jata (matted locks tied once at the crown), a full white beard, skin that is very dark brown and smooth for his age. Bark-cloth robe in pale cream-grey. No ornament of any kind except a simple string of rudraksha beads at the throat. He walks barefoot with a calm gait that says he is in no hurry, ever. His eyes are entirely at peace — this is a man who has seen a great deal and judges nothing. He carries a wooden water pot when at the river; a bundle of freshly gathered fruit when returning from the forest.
