# Character Passport: Shakuntala

**Role:** Daughter of Viswamitra and Menaka, raised by the sage Kanwa; central figure of S1E01 scenes 09–17.
**Appears in:** `s1e01_the_scribe` sc02 (H06–H10), sc03 (V05–V18)

---

## Descriptor

Shakuntala, daughter of the apsara Menaka and the sage Viswamitra, raised in the hermitage of Kanwa beside the river Malini. A young woman, mid-twenties, with an apsara's grace and a hermitage-girl's calm. Slim build, skin the warm brown of someone who has lived in the forest all her life, dark wavy hair worn loosely with fresh forest flowers woven in. Bark-cloth outer robe over plain cream cotton, bound with a simple vine cord at the waist. No silk, no jewellery beyond small copper earrings and a river-stone bracelet — only what a hermitage gives. Bare feet. Her posture is relaxed in her own space, tall and direct when addressing the king. Her face shows composure first, then hurt, then righteous authority — it must never tip into either submissiveness or melodrama.

## Physical Description

- **Age:** Mid-twenties
- **Height:** Medium, graceful build
- **Skin:** Warm forest brown
- **Hair:** Dark, wavy, loose; fresh forest flowers (white and yellow) woven in
- **Eyes:** Large, dark, direct gaze
- **Marks:** None
- **Costume:** Bark-cloth robe over cream cotton dhoti, vine-cord belt, bare feet, small copper earrings, river-stone bracelet
- **She carries:** A clay water pot in hermitage scenes; her son Bharata's hand in court scenes

## Modelling Notes

- Design entirely original — no reference to Kalidasa's Shakuntala or any existing film/TV version.
- The ring, the curse of Durvasa, and Shakuntala's forgetting are from Kalidasa's play — do NOT design any symbol or costume element that references that version.
- Bark-cloth: a layered outer garment of dull tan/grey natural fibres, slightly textured, not shiny.
- Her flowers: not formal garlands — single flowers tucked into her hair as she found them.
- Sheet needed: front/side/back orthographic in hermitage costume (H06) and court scene (V12).
