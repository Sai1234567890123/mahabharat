# Arjuna and Karna: the sinking wheel: script

No dialogue. Read it top to bottom as one piece of action; each line is one shot.

## The challenge (0 to 16 s)

**S01** (0 s) Two chariots roll toward each other across the empty plain. *Low wind. Two conches answer each other, far apart.*

**S02** (4 s) Arjuna's chariot runs left to right, Krishna calm at the reins. *Hooves, wheel rumble, cloth snapping.*

**S03** (7 s) Karna's chariot runs right to left, the sun behind him. *Heavier hooves, a deeper wheel, one low drum.*

**S04** (10 s) Arjuna closes his eyes for one breath, opens them and raises Gandiva. *Everything drops to one held breath. Then a bowstring creaks.*

**S05** (13 s) Karna looks up at the sun, and the sun medallion on his chest flares. *A low hum rises. One bell.*

## The arrow storm (16 to 40 s)

**S06** (16 s) Both archers draw and loose at the same instant. *Two bowstrings snap as one. A rising whistle.*

**S07** (20 s) The two arrows meet point to point in the middle of the field and burst. *Silence for half a second, then one crack like a struck anvil.*

**S08** (23 s) Karna looses a fire weapon: one arrow becomes a wall of flame. *A roar like a furnace door opening.*

**S09** (27 s) Arjuna answers with a rain weapon and the wall of flame goes out. *Thunder, then hard rain, then hiss and steam.*

**S10** (31 s) From above, the two chariots circle each other, trading arrows. *Wheels grinding in a circle. Arrows hiss both ways.*

**S11** (35 s) The camera flies with a stream of Arjuna's arrows as Karna cuts them down one after another. *A fast run of drum hits, one per arrow. Metal on metal.*

## The turn (40 to 64 s)

**S12** (40 s) An orange arrow cuts Arjuna's bowstring. *A single high snap. Then nothing.*

**S13** (43 s) Krishna glances back over his shoulder, calm, and gives the smallest nod. *Wind. A soft flute note, the only one in the film.*

**S14** (46 s) Karna draws one special arrow from a golden case. A fiery serpent coils along it. *A dry rattle. A hiss that rises in pitch.*

**S15** (50 s) The serpent arrow crosses the field, burning a line through the dust. *A long rising scream of air. Drums stop.*

**S16** (54 s) Krishna presses his foot down. The chariot sinks a hand's depth into the earth and the horses kneel. *One deep thud, felt more than heard. Horses snort.*

**S17** (58 s) The serpent arrow misses his head and takes the diadem. *A bell struck once, very loud. Then ringing.*

**S18** (61 s) Arjuna rises bare-headed, ties a white cloth on his brow, and lightning climbs the restrung bow. *Ringing fades. Thunder rolls back in, closer.*

## The wheel (64 to 90 s)

**S19** (64 s) Karna's left wheel sinks into the earth and the chariot lurches to a stop. *Wood groans. The wheel rumble stops dead.*

**S20** (68 s) On foot, Karna strains to lift the wheel with both hands, then looks up at the sun. *His breathing. Far away, a conch.*

**S21** (72 s) Arjuna at full draw, eyes closed for one breath. Krishna nods once. *The bow creaks. One breath. No music.*

**S22** (75 s) He looses. A blue impact frame. *The loudest bowstring in the film. Then a thunderclap.*

**S23** (78 s) The arrow crosses the field. Karna turns to face it. White light. *The scream of the arrow, cut to total silence on the flash.*

**S24** (82 s) A column of gold light rises from where Karna stood and enters the sun. Arjuna lowers his bow. *One long conch. Wind. The music bed resolves and stops.*
