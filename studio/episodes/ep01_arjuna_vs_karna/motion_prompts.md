# The Anjalika: motion prompts (image-to-video)

The scene lives in the frame. These prompts are motion only: one action, one camera move, timed in beats. Render each at 5 to 8 s and trim to the card length in the edit.

## A01

```
Image-to-video, 5 seconds, from the attached still.
0-2s: both arrowheads tremble a hair apart, sparks drift.
2-3s: they touch; a 2-frame white impact frame.
3-4s: snap zoom out, the burst becomes blue and orange rings.
Camera: Extreme close-up, locked off, then a 6-frame snap zoom out.
Sound: Silence, then one hard crack. No music.
Painterly stylized animation, 2D effects on twos, no text, the characters do not speak.
```
