## The episode in one line

Vyasa has made the whole epic in his mind and has no one to write it down. Brahma sends him Ganesha, and the dictation begins with the story of how the Bharata line got its name: Shakuntala, a king who forgets his promise, and a voice from heaven that makes him keep it.

## Notes on things people expect but Ganguli does not have

- **Ganesha breaking his tusk to write.** This is not in this text; it comes from later retellings. **sai decided on 2026-11-26 to keep it.** In scene 04 it is marked **added**: when his pen fails mid-dictation, Ganesha breaks off his tusk and writes on with it, so the dictation never stops. This fits his own condition.
- **The ring, the curse of Durvasa, and Shakuntala's forgetting.** These are from Kalidasa's play, not the Mahabharata. Here the king forgets nothing; he denies her on purpose. Do not add them.
- **Ganesha's own presence in the epic.** The Ganesha passage is in the vulgate text that Ganguli translated. Some later critical editions leave it out, so a viewer may say "this is not in the Mahabharata". The answer is that it is in Ganguli, lines 461-483, which is our source.

## Period and look checklist for this episode

The checklist from the teaser (`episodes/ep01_arjuna_vs_karna/canon_check.md`) still applies. Add these:

- [ ] **Hermitages:** thatched huts, fire altars, deer and sacred trees, a river. No stone temples or carved idols, because the age is earlier than that.
- [ ] **Writing:** Ganesha writes on palm leaves or birch bark with a stylus or pen, as Ganguli's "pen" says. No paper books and no bound volumes.
- [ ] **Brahma:** four faces, with a lotus seat. He appears in light and does not walk in. Keep the locked symmetrical frame for both Brahma and Ganesha.
- [ ] **Dushmanta's army:** chariots, elephants, horses and foot soldiers, with bows and spears. Same rule as the teaser: no stirrups, no saddles with horns, nothing machined.
- [ ] **Shakuntala:** bark-cloth and simple cotton, flowers, no jewellery beyond what a hermitage gives. Her look must be designed new; there is no locked sheet.
- [ ] **Animals:** vultures in scene 11 are protectors. Paint them noble, not horror.
