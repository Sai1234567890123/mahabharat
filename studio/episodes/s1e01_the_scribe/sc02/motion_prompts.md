# The Scribe: The Hunter King (Scenes 07–09): motion prompts (image-to-video)

The scene lives in the frame. These prompts are motion only: one action, one camera move, timed in beats. Render each at 5 to 8 s and trim to the card length in the edit.

```

4-7s: @dushmanta turns to scan the forest ahead, one hand raised to signal the hunt.
```
