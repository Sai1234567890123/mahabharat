# Scene 14 "The King Who Forgot": shortlist of Shakuntala's lines

Her answer to Dushmanta runs from B01 line 9072 to 9279, about 200 lines of the Ganguli text. Scene 14 has room for **3 or 4 lines**, about 25 seconds of speech.

Below are 10 candidates, quoted exactly from Ganguli with their line numbers. They are grouped by the job each one does in the scene.

**sai: pick 3 or 4 by number.** The recommended set is at the end.

## 1. The claim (she arrives)

1. "This is thy son, O king! Let him be installed as thy heir-apparent." (9072)
2. "Call to mind, O thou of great good fortune, the agreement thou hadst made on the occasion of thy union with me in the asylum of Kanwa." (9075-9077)

## 2. The rebuke (after he denies her)

3. "Knowing everything, O monarch, how thou, like an inferior person, thus say that thou knowest it not? Thy heart is a witness to the truth or falsehood of this matter." (9091-9094)
4. "Thou thinkest that thou alone hast knowledge of thy deed. But knowest thou not that the Ancient, Omniscient one liveth in thy heart?" (9096-9098)
5. "The Sun, the Moon, the Air, the Fire, the Earth, the Sky, Water, the heart, Yama, the day, the night, both twilights, and Dharma, all witness the acts of man." (9101-9103)
6. "In the presence of so many, why dost thou treat me like an ordinary woman? I am not certainly crying in the wilderness. Dost thou not hear me?" (9111-9113)

## 3. The wound (her own story)

7. "What sinful act did I do, of old, in some other life that I was in infancy cast away by my parents and at present am cast away by thee!" (9189-9191)

## 4. The answer to his insult (after he calls her mother lewd)

8. "Thou seest, O king, the fault of others, even though they be as small as a mustard seed. But seeing, thou noticest not thy own faults even though they be as large as the Vilwa fruit." (9218-9220)

## 5. Truth (the turn that brings the voice from heaven)

9. "A hundred horse-sacrifices had once been weighed against Truth, and Truth was found heavier than a hundred horse-sacrifices." (9266-9268)
10. "Truth is God himself; Truth is the highest vow. Therefore, violate not thy pledge, O monarch! ... this son of mine shall rule the whole Earth surrounded by the four seas." (9269-9276)

## Recommended set (about 25 seconds)

**1, 3, 7 and 10**, in that order.

- **1** is the claim.
- **3** is her rebuke, and "thy heart is a witness" sets up the voice from heaven.
- **7** carries the emotional weight, because it links her abandonment as a baby to this second abandonment.
- **10** ends on truth and her son's future. The voice from heaven in scene 15 then proves her right.

If one line has to go, cut 7 and keep the scene about truth.

## Notes

- **Wording:** the quotes are Ganguli's words, unchanged. For the voice track, "thou" stays because it suits the period. Any modern rewording would be marked **dramatised** in the canon check.
- **Dushmanta's insult:** it is cut down to one line in scene 14: "I don't know thee. Go withersoever thou choosest." (end of his speech, 9213-9214)
