## From the text (Ganguli translation, public domain)

- **Standards (Drona Parva, section CIV):** Arjuna's standard bears "the ape of fierce face and tail, like that of the
  lion" (Hanuman). Karna's standard "bore the mark of an elephant-rope made of gold", with a banner "adorned with gold
  and garlands". Shalya's standard-top carries an image like the presiding goddess of corn.
  [sacred-texts.com, Drona Parva CIV](https://sacred-texts.com/hin/m07/m07101.htm)
- **Karna:** born with the natural armour and earrings (kavacha and kundala) from Surya, which he gave away to Indra
  before the war, so in battle he has neither. Bow: Vijaya, given by Parashurama (traditions differ on its origin).
- **Arjuna:** bow Gandiva and two inexhaustible quivers (from Varuna at the Khandava fire), conch Devadatta, diadem from
  Indra (the name Kiriti, "the crowned one"), chariot drawn by white horses (Shvetavahana).

## What the paintings add

- **Karna (Pahari):** a red triangular banner with an elephant on it, a white parasol over the chariot, four white horses,
  a tall crown with a black feather plume, full black beard, orange and gold armour, a black round shield on his back.
  Shalya drives, also crowned and bearded.
- **Arjuna and Krishna (Pahari):** Arjuna stands behind with the bow, crowned with a plume; blue Krishna drives in front;
  white horses; a parasol here too.
- **Karna at the wheel (Ravi Varma):** Karna bent low with both hands on the rim of the sunken wheel, red dhoti, gold
  armour and crown, the horses rearing, Arjuna's chariot in the distance. This is the classic staging of shot A16.

## Differences from our current designs (for sai to decide)

1. **Karna's banner:** ours is plain gold. Text and painting both say a golden elephant-rope emblem (painting: an elephant).
2. **Arjuna's banner:** ours is a green ape banner in concept art; the text says the ape (Hanuman) itself on the standard.
3. **Parasols:** our rule is "no parasol on any chariot"; the traditional paintings show a white parasol (chhatra) on
   both chariots. Keeping our rule is a style choice, not a canon point.
4. **Horses:** Arjuna's white horses are canon. Our Karna has dun horses; the Pahari painting gives him white horses too.
5. **Beards:** both paintings give Karna and Shalya full beards; our Karna has a moustache only (sai kept the v2 look).
6. **Earrings:** canon agrees with our "no earrings in battle" rule for Karna.
