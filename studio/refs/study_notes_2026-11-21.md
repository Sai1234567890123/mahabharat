# Style study: sai's references for Arjuna, Karna and Hastinapur (2026-11-21)

sai shared four phone screenshots. They are other people's work (Instagram artists and a TV series still), so the
images stay on sai's PC only, in `studio/refs/study_reels/ref_*.jpg` (gitignored, the repo is public), and they are
never sent to an image model. These notes describe what to take from them in our own words.

## Arjuna: "ARJUNA" key art by naveenconcepts (Instagram)
- Pose: mid-air, one knee raised, full draw across the body, low camera, bow diagonal through the frame.
- Palette: night blue and steel silver, white-blue lightning wrapping the bow arm and trailing off the arrow.
- Costume read: silver-grey armour (not gold) with fine engraving, deep blue dhoti, a crescent-shaped silver crown
  ornament, long loose hair blown back, a single ear stud.
- Take for us: Arjuna's colour key is blue-white lightning on cool steel; dynamic leaping archery poses.

## Karna: "KARNA" key art by naveenconcepts (Instagram)
- Pose: full draw, head down in shadow, hair wild, three-quarter view, the bow almost blending into fire.
- Palette: molten orange, ember red and gold; the body glows from inside like lava; a glowing sun disc on the chest.
- Costume read: dark bronze-black armour with glowing seams, the sun emblem as a light source rather than a carving.
- Take for us: Karna's colour key is fire and sun-gold on dark metal; the chest sun can glow during his astra shots.

## Arjuna: TV series still (Google image result)
- Silver-and-gold engraved armour, a tall filigree crown, bracers, rope belt; a giant gold chariot and wheel behind.
- Shows the "royal armour" density sai likes; we must not copy the actor's likeness or the series' costume one to one.

## Hastinapur and world: naveen.selvan and treycochran3d (Instagram)
- naveen.selvan "Immortal Realm in the Himalayas": a painted fantasy city of stacked temples and towers on cliffs,
  waterfalls, magenta bougainvillea curtains in the foreground, a rainbow, elephants in silhouette, pastel morning light.
- treycochran3d: a colossal canyon with two giant carved guardian statues, god rays through mist, a tiny figure for scale.
- Take for us: Hastinapur as a vertical, layered palace city with waterfalls and flowering foreground framing; giant
  carved guardian statues at the gates for scale shots.

## Proposed series colour keys (for sai to approve)
- Karna: fire, molten orange and sun-gold on dark bronze.
- Arjuna: blue-white lightning on silver-steel and deep blue.
- Krishna: soft lavender-blue and gold with a calm warm glow.
- Hastinapur: pastel morning light, magenta flowers, white stone and gold domes.
