sai: "Take these references for arjuna and karna and hastinapur". Four phone screenshots. They are other people's work
(and one TV still), so they are STUDY ONLY: never attached to a model, never committed to this public repo, never copied.

## 1. TV still: Arjuna from a television series

- Real actor. Face and likeness: NOT usable in any form (bible, Never).
- Costume idea worth keeping: armour layered from several metals (silver-grey plates with gold trim, not all gold), thick
  twisted rope cords across the chest and waist, heavy cuffed bracers. It makes the armour read as worn war gear.
- Use: a later armour detail pass on Arjuna could add silver-grey plates and rope cords. Not changing his locked look now.

## 2. Painted fantasy landscape ("Immortal Realm") plus a canyon with colossal statues (for Hastinapur)

- Look: painted matte, soft pastel light (pink, lilac, gold), hanging magenta flowers in the foreground as a frame,
  temple towers stacked up a slope, waterfalls, a faint rainbow, layers that fade into haze. Loose brushwork, no
  photographic texture.
- Statue canyon: colossal carved guardian statues dwarf one tiny figure, light shafts from above, deep cool shadows. Pure
  scale storytelling.
- Use for Hastinapur and the series' painted backgrounds: foreground frame of flowers or carvings, a mid-ground city
  stacked on terraces, far layers fading to pastel haze, one tiny figure for scale. Matches the bible's painted-matte
  rule.

## 3. Karna key art: fire astra moment

- Dark smoky background; the hero is lit almost only by his own energy. Fire wraps the whole body as ribbons and
  strands, embers fly, hair whips in the energy wind. A sun emblem on the chest burns brightest (the focal point).
- Colour: black, ember red, orange, gold-white core. Matches our Karna astra colours (#FFF1C2 core, #FF8A1E rim).
- Use: Karna's astra shots (the volley, the Brahmastra attempt): drop the ambient light, let the sun-face on his cuirass
  ignite first, fire ribbons spiral up the arms into the arrow.

## 4. Arjuna key art: lightning astra moment

- Night-blue sky and smoke; white-blue lightning ribbons run along the arrow and wrap the body; Arjuna mid-leap, bow at
  full draw, cloth flowing. Silver crown with a ring halo behind the head. Cool palette only, white-hot core.
- Matches our Arjuna astra colours (#F2FAFF core, #3FA9FF rim).
- Use: Arjuna's astra shots: blue night grade, lightning ribbons along the arrow, a halo-ring glow behind his Kiriti
  (light effect only; his crown design stays locked).

## Rules these add to the bible (web series look)

- Astra moments: the hero is lit by his own energy against a dark, smoky ground; energy wraps the body as ribbons; one
  emblem (Karna's sun-face, Arjuna's arrowhead) is the brightest point.
- Painted worlds: framed foregrounds, terraced cities, pastel haze layers, tiny figures against colossal forms.
