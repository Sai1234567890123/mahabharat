# Plan the duel like Baahubali, render like Flow

**Short answer.** The big epics in every medium follow the same order of work. They lock the film as pictures first: 3,500 boards on *Fury Road*, previs on *Game of Thrones*. They write one style guide that every artist follows. They spend most of their effort on a few hero moments and keep the rest simple. Then they finish the look in compositing. The stylized 3D hits that sai's look comes from (*Arcane*, *Spider-Verse*, *Puss in Boots*, *Baahubali: The Eternal War*) break CG on purpose. They use stepped keys, painted textures, hand-drawn 2D effects and deliberate imperfection. The Mahabharata adaptations that lasted, above all B.R. Chopra's 1988 serial, won on performance, iconography and clear staging, not effects. The ones that failed (the 2013 animated film, the 2025 AI series) failed on caricature faces, lip sync, dull battles and period errors that viewers screenshotted. sai's repo already runs the right family of pipeline: painted 3D characters, Blender, Grease Pencil effects and Unreal plates. It is close to *Arcane*, *Schirkoa* and *Flow*. Five changes matter most. Grade every shot S/A/B/C before animating. Move final rendering off CPU-only Cycles to EEVEE, with the free SheepIt farm or a $0.50/hour rented GPU as backup. Build a reusable 2D effects library. Add a colour script and a period-accuracy pass. Keep AI video out of EP01. The rest of this report shows the evidence and gives a step-by-step plan with tools, costs and owners.

A note on evidence. The first-pass notes ran out of fetch budget, and a later verification pass rechecked the weak claims. Where the verification changed a claim, this report uses the corrected version. Claims that only one unnamed source supports are marked "reported" or "unconfirmed".

## How the big productions are made, step by step

Every production below, whether live action, 3D, 2D or AI, goes through the same eleven steps. They differ in where they put people and money. Each step lists named examples, then what it means for sai.

### 1. Development and adaptation: one image, one arc, one canon keeper

Baahubali writer K.V. Vijayendra Prasad starts from a single vivid image. *Baahubali* grew from a picture of an injured woman holding a baby above rough water. He then checks a complete beginning, middle and end by telling it aloud. He also splits his writers into teams that argue for and against the script ([Film Companion](https://www.filmcompanion.in/features/inside-the-mind-of-kv-vijayendra-prasad-the-writer-of-baahubali-and-the-much-awaited-rrr-ss-rajamouli-telugu-movies)). *The Eternal War* hired a mythology consultant, Vinay Varanasi, to keep the devas, asuras and 14 lokas close to scripture while the plot is invented ([Hollywood Reporter India](https://www.hollywoodreporterindia.com/amp/story/features/interviews/exclusive-ishan-shukla-on-prabhas-starring-animated-epic-baahubali-the-eternal-war-and-working-with-ss-rajamouli)). In the AI world, Ek Dharmayudh's maker stressed that the team of about 130 to 140 included writers, directors and sketch artists. He called the work "not mindless prompting" ([THR India](https://www.hollywoodreporterindia.com/features/interviews/vijay-subramaniam-on-ai-mahabharata-anurag-kashyaps-critique-and-future-of-mid-tier-films-they-will-go-into-oblivion)). For sai, the Ganguli database is the canon keeper. EP01's image is already chosen: Karna's light entering the sun while Arjuna does not celebrate.

### 2. Look development: one style bible, a few "key frames"

On *300*, VFX art director Grant Freckelton wrote a Photoshop style guide for every vendor. Frank Miller panels that had to appear on screen were flagged as "Frank frames" and matched for composition and light ([American Cinematographer](https://theasc.com/articles/few-against-many-300)). Pixar plans colour as a "colour script": one emotional colour per sequence, set before production ([Pixar in a Box, reported](https://pixar.com/pixar-in-a-box)). *The Eternal War*'s director calls his look "painterly 3D". He says, "We make it seem like it's hand-painted, but it also provides the flexibility of moving the camera around." He avoids hyper-realism because "that ages really fast" ([HR India](https://www.hollywoodreporterindia.com/features/interviews/exclusive-ishan-shukla-on-prabhas-starring-animated-epic-baahubali-the-eternal-war-and-working-with-ss-rajamouli); [Cartoon Brew](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/feature-film/baahubali-the-eternal-war-rajamouli-annecy-263875.html)). His earlier Star Wars short, *The Bandits of Golak*, took its oil-paint look from Raja Ravi Varma's brushwork, with the rule that any paused frame should work as a painting ([Film Companion](https://www.filmcompanion.in/interviews/hollywood-interview/star-wars-how-the-first-indian-entry-to-the-franchise-was-made-star-wars-visions-season-2-disney-hotstar-the-bandits-of-golak)). sai's looks are locked. What is missing is the written bible and a colour script. A colour script is 6 to 8 painted frames that fix the palette for each part of the episode.

### 3. Previs and layout: the action is designed before anyone animates it

George Miller and Brendan McCarthy drew about 3,500 storyboards for *Fury Road*. The boards worked as the script, and more than half the final shots matched them closely ([No Film School](https://nofilmschool.com/mad-max-fury-road-was-filmed-without-finished-script)). On *Game of Thrones*' "Battle of the Bastards", the VFX supervisor said, "The sequence was all in the preplanning" ([The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/how-game-of-thrones-emmy-award-winning-battle-scene-was-made-65235)). In anime, MAPPA built the roughly five-second "blown away" cut in *Chainsaw Man – Reze Arc* as a 3D movie at layout stage, "with the understanding that it would be replaced by hand-drawn animation" ([80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/see-how-denji-vs-reze-fight-animation-sequence-from-chainsaw-man-the-movie-reze-arc-was-turned-from-3d-to-2d)). Ishan Shukla's *Schirkoa* feature used Unreal 3D layout to make its animatic ([befores & afters](https://beforesandafters.com/2020/08/31/schirkoa-director-ishan-shukla-on-moving-to-a-real-time-workflow/)). sai already has this step: shots.json, an HTML animatic, layout.blend and cameras.json. The professional habit to add is locking the edit, with temp sound, before final animation starts.

### 4. Models and rigs: few heroes, many cheap copies

The productions that scale do not build everything at hero quality. *Chainsaw Man*'s CG team made three detail levels for each main character (long, medium, close). The hero model took four to five weeks ([CGWorld](https://cgworld.jp/article/202303-chainsawman1.html)). On *RRR*, Makuta VFX ran its whole 3D pipeline except effects in Blender. It used Geometry Nodes to scatter foliage by rule and built crowd assets in Blender ([Blender.org](https://www.blender.org/?p=83163)). Hair was the hardest single problem on *The Eternal War* ([Cartoon Brew](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/feature-film/baahubali-the-eternal-war-rajamouli-annecy-263875.html)). Indian film's cautionary case is the 2013 animated *Mahabharat*. It designed characters to look like their star voice actors, and critics called the animation "immature" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahabharat_(2013_film))). sai's hero rigs (Arjuna v3, Krishna v2, Karna v3) and the battlefield of about 14,000 instances already follow the "few heroes, many copies" rule. The known weak spots are hands, faces and the realist TRELLIS surface. Those are repaint and repair jobs, not rebuilds.

### 5. Animation: frame rate is a creative choice

The stylized films vary frame rate on purpose, but not the way people often repeat. *Into the Spider-Verse* ran mostly on twos because the team disliked "the smoothness of being on 1s". *Across the Spider-Verse* went "sometimes 1s, sometimes 2s or 3s, right up to 6s" ([Cartoon Brew](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/feature-film/if-its-not-broke-break-it-sony-imageworks-renegade-approach-to-spider-man-into-the-spider-verse-167321.html); [befores & afters](https://beforesandafters.com/2023/06/28/this-was-the-first-cg-animated-movie-ive-ever-heard-of-that-actually-had-a-dedicated-inking-team/)). *Puss in Boots: The Last Wish* is "mostly on ones". Only the big action goes "to 2s or 4s", and smear frames appear in "probably only… 10 shots" ([befores & afters](https://beforesandafters.com/2023/01/11/heres-how-dreamworks-lent-a-whole-new-brand-of-stylized-look-and-feel-to-puss-in-boots-the-last-wish)). Arc System Works made 3D read as anime in *Guilty Gear Xrd* by turning interpolation off. "Every frame now is a key frame." The team also deformed the mesh on every key "to add imperfection" ([GDC slides](https://www.ggxrd.com/Motomura_Junya_GuiltyGearXrd.pdf)). In Demon Slayer, an analysis by J.B. Siraudin finds most action on twos, strikes often a single frame, and a "prepare long, strike short, relax long" rhythm ([Siraudin](https://jbsiraudin.github.io/blog/demon-slayer-visual-grammar)). On mocap, the record is mixed. Shukla used consumer Perception Neuron suits and iPhone face capture for secondary motion on *Schirkoa*, then polished hero motion by hand ([befores & afters](https://beforesandafters.com/2020/08/31/schirkoa-director-ishan-shukla-on-moving-to-a-real-time-workflow/)). *Arjun: The Warrior Prince* ran a two-week Kalaripayattu and Thang-Ta workshop for its crew ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arjun:_The_Warrior_Prince)). The often-quoted claim that *Arcane* was "keyframe, not mocap" traces to one unattributed article only. sai's hand keys on twos, scripted through anim_lib.py and moves.py, are a proven approach. Push only the release frames to ones, and hold the anticipation poses longer.

### 6. FX: hand-drawn energy over 3D bodies

This is where the painterly films agree most strongly. Fortiche's art director on *Arcane* says, "we use a mix of 2D and 3D techniques". Season 2 chose 2D for smoke and fire ([VFX Voice](https://vfxvoice.com/riot-games-and-fortiche-get-revolutionary-with-arcane-season-2/)). On *Puss*, "the magic secret sauce on top were hand-drawn elements", and the wolf fight's dust and impacts were "all-2D" ([befores & afters](https://beforesandafters.com/2023/01/11/heres-how-dreamworks-lent-a-whole-new-brand-of-stylized-look-and-feel-to-puss-in-boots-the-last-wish)). On *Across the Spider-Verse*, the 2D effects team grew to six animators drawing shot by shot in Toon Boom Harmony. The first film had used a reusable effects library instead ([Toon Boom](https://www.toonboom.com/?p=16859)). *The Eternal War* adds "sakuga-style anime" for the god fights, and Studio Khara draws one sequence fully in 2D ([Cartoon Brew](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/feature-film/baahubali-the-eternal-war-rajamouli-annecy-263875.html)). Sakuga means standout, highly expressive hand-drawn animation. In live action, *Dune* colour-coded shield hits, blue when deflected and red when they got through ([befores & afters](https://beforesandafters.com/2021/11/06/the-dune-visual-effects-team-used-sandscreens-instead-of-bluescreens/)). The industrial extreme is *Ne Zha 2*. Its waterfall fight was rendered on a 50-workstation cluster at 32 hours per frame ([Shenzhen Daily](http://www.szdaily.com/content/2025-03/03/content_31477678.htm)). That is the clearest example of what a solo creator must not copy. sai's two-colour astra code (Karna sun-gold, Arjuna white and lightning-blue) is exactly the *Dune* idea. The A06 Grease Pencil volley test is the right method.

### 7. Lighting and render: real-time engines won the small productions

*Flow*, which won the Oscar, "was made entirely with Blender and rendered with EEVEE". Each 4K frame took about 0.5 to 10 seconds on the director's own PC. "We didn't use a renderfarm." "There was no compositing, all the colors were tweaked… using shaders." About 15 to 20 people worked on it over about five and a half years, with three to five at any time ([blender.org](https://www.blender.org/?p=93732)), on a budget of about $3.8 million ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91232740/flow-movie-turned-a-4-million-budget-into-an-animated-work-of-art)). Netflix's *Super Giant Robot Brothers* was made entirely in Unreal and "filmed" an episode every two weeks ([AWN](https://www.awn.com/animationworld/super-giant-robot-brothers-milestone-virtual-animation-production)). Bigger productions render in passes so they can change things later. On *Kalki 2898 AD*, Granite Imagery rendered Cycles with separate light passes, data passes and Cryptomatte so fixes could be made in compositing without re-rendering. Its heaviest frames went to a cloud farm ([BlenderNation](https://blendernation.com/2024/09/14/behind-the-scenes-of-kalki-how-we-used-blender-to-create-stunning-vfx-in-our-first-major-project)). Klaus faked 3D light on 2D drawings with "Klaus Light and Shadow", using up to eight drawn lighting layers merged into a "baked" look ([befores & afters](https://beforesandafters.com/2019/11/14/heres-what-made-the-2d-animation-in-klaus-look-3d/)). For sai, the lesson is blunt. The current CPU-only Cycles path, which took about 2 hours 10 minutes for 48 seconds at 960x540, is the opposite of what the small winners did.

### 8. Compositing ("photography"): the cheapest place to unify a look

In Japanese anime, compositing is called "photography". ufotable's digital chief Yuichi Terao says, "if you do the lighting correctly, the characters will blend into the backgrounds no matter what style they're drawn in" ([Anime News Network](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/feature/2018-01-10/interview-yuichi-terao-chief-of-ufotable-digital-team/.125658)). *Arcane*'s backgrounds start as a 3D set. Artists then paint over it and project the paint back onto the 3D assets, so the camera can move with parallax ([80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/arcane-artists-show-how-they-combine-traditional-art-3d-for-backgrounds)). Parallax means near things move faster than far things, which makes a flat painting feel deep. *Spider-Verse* replaced depth of field with "a color off-set process" that shifts scenes out of focus ([Cartoon Brew](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/feature-film/if-its-not-broke-break-it-sony-imageworks-renegade-approach-to-spider-man-into-the-spider-verse-167321.html)). *300* got its graphic look from "the Crush": clipped highlights, crushed shadows and desaturation ([American Cinematographer](https://theasc.com/articles/few-against-many-300)). Blender's compositor has a Kuwahara filter, a paint-like smoothing effect. Its manual suggests downscale, filter, then upscale for speed ([Blender manual](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/dev/compositing/types/creative/kuwahara.html)). Blender's planned "NPR" (non-photoreal) rendering feature has changed direction. It is now "Multi-stage Compositing", meaning per-object compositor trees, and no shipped release was confirmed ([Blender devtalk](https://devtalk.blender.org/t/npr-project-multi-stage-compositing/40758/1)). So sai should build with stock EEVEE, Grease Pencil and the compositor now, and not wait for it.

### 9. Sound and music: the voice is the theme

Hans Zimmer built *Dune*'s score around the human voice and deliberately "misused" instruments ([No Film School](https://nofilmschool.com/zimmer-dune-score)). M.M. Keeravani, who is scoring *The Eternal War*, made "Saahore Baahubali" a war-cry anthem ([Scroll](https://scroll.in/reel/833204/keeravani-in-telugu-and-mm-kreem-in-hindi-tune-into-the-baahubali-music-composer)). In animation, *The Legend of Hanuman* "use[s] animatics for allowing actors to act instead of just dubbing" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Legend_of_Hanuman)). *Arjun* recorded its voices as a stage play ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arjun:_The_Warrior_Prince)). By contrast, critics of the 2013 Star Plus *Mahabharat* called its background music too loud ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahabharat_(2013_TV_series))). For sai, the code-made sound is a strong base and is clearly human-authored. The conch is a natural leitmotif.

### 10. Edit: edit while you make it

*Ponniyin Selvan* used a spot editor who cut war scenes on set during the shoot (reported, [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/amp/story/entertainment/cinema/spot-editor-of-ponniyin-selvan-opens-up-on-mani-ratnam-reaction-to-a-war-scene-edit)). *Fury Road*'s editor won the Oscar for cutting a film whose shot count nearly matched its boards ([No Film School](https://nofilmschool.com/mad-max-fury-road-was-filmed-without-finished-script)). *Baahubali 2* shows the cost of skipping this. Two sequences were still unshot when the release date was fixed, and the VFX supervisor said three to four more months would have let him fine-tune every shot ([The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/flix/baahubali-2-high-emotions-not-just-visual-effects-tnm-exclusive-films-vfx-supervisor-60898)). For sai, every blocked shot should drop straight into the animatic timeline, and compositing, grading and sound get their own scheduled days.

### 11. Release: a free pilot first, then growth

*Hazbin Hotel* began as a Patreon-funded pilot on its creator's YouTube channel in 2019 and premiered on Prime Video in January 2024 ([Paste](https://www.pastemagazine.com/comedy/hazbin-hotel/from-patreon-to-prime-video-vivienne-medranos-road-to-the-hazbin-hotel)). *The Amazing Digital Circus* pilot passed 350 million views. Glitch chose YouTube because it "was just the biggest reach" ([YouTube Official Blog](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/amazing-digital-circus/)). *Hanu-Man*'s director said an early released creative turned viewers into "partner[s] in the making" ([Outlook India](https://www.outlookindia.com/art-entertainment/-hanuman-director-prasanth-varma-reveals-how-the-film-is-made-on-modest-budget-news-342834)). Ishan Shukla himself went from a low-budget indie feature to directing a ₹120 crore film ([HR India](https://www.hollywoodreporterindia.com/features/interviews/exclusive-ishan-shukla-on-prabhas-starring-animated-epic-baahubali-the-eternal-war-and-working-with-ss-rajamouli)). sai's route of short episodes, a vertical cut, and a free release on sai's own channel is the proven indie path.

### How the 1988 serial staged the Karna duel

Episode 89, "Karna Dies", aired on 10 June 1990 and ran 45 minutes. The duel begins at sunrise. Karna fires an arrow that "splits fire" toward Arjuna. Krishna pushes Arjuna's chariot "five fingers deep in the mud", so the shaft takes only his crown ([TV Episode Guides](https://tvepguides.com/shows/mahabharat-1988/episodes/24825961/s01e89-karna-dies)). Later, Karna's wheel is "submerged" and he tries to lift it. Krishna reminds Arjuna of Abhimanyu's death and the Kauravas' broken rules, and Arjuna beheads Karna. Kunti then reveals that Karna was their eldest brother ([Bollywood Hungama](https://test-stag.bollywoodhungama.com/news/features/mahabharat-trends-twitter-netizens-get-emotional-seeing-pankaj-dheers-karnas-death-scene)). Two details matter for sai. First, the 1988 episode guide describes a fire arrow, not the serpent arrow in Ganguli. sai's serpent arrow is therefore more faithful to the text, and it is visually distinct from the best-known screen version. Second, the 1988 serial shows the death directly. sai's "no gore" rule fits the Anjalika beat better if it is staged as light and silhouette, which is what A19 to A21 already do. No source describes how the 2013 Star Plus episode (1x252, "Arjun kills Karna", aired 30 July 2014) staged the duel ([TVmaze](https://www.tvmaze.com/episodes/314272/mahabharat-1x252-arjun-kills-karna)). The same is true of *Suryaputra Karn* and the 2019 *Kurukshetra*.

### Netflix's Kurukshetra is the closest competitor, and it leaves room

Netflix calls *Kurukshetra* "the first mythological anime from Netflix India". It has 18 episodes, each told from one key warrior's point of view across the 18 days. Part 1 came out on 10 October 2025 and Part 2 on 24 October 2025. Gulzar wrote the lyrics and narration ([Netflix](http://about.netflix.com/en/news/netflix-marks-foray-into-indian-animated-mythology-with-kurukshetra); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurukshetra:_The_Great_War_of_Mahabharata)). Reviewers praised its ambition but called the animation "uneven" and the voices weak. Scroll said it "relies heavily on spectacle". No source names its software. Its Karna episode is not described anywhere in the notes. The same producers, Alok Jain and Ajit Andhare, also back the AI film *Chiranjeevi Hanuman*. That film missed its Hanuman Jayanti 2026 date, and its release by late November 2026 is unconfirmed ([Variety India](https://varietyindia.com/mythological-slates-expand-chiranjeevi-hanuman-the-eternal-to-release-after-ranbir-kapoors-ramayana-exclusive/)). sai cannot beat Netflix on scale, but can differ in four ways: close fidelity to Ganguli's beats, a painterly look instead of anime, 60–90 second episodes with vertical cuts, and free release. The Mahavatar Krishna films are dated 2031 and 2033, so they will not compete in the next two years ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahavatar_Narsimha)).

### Seven lessons from the Indian adaptations

Show divinity through stillness. The 1988 Vishwaroop used a locked camera, held poses and layered passes, and the actor held poses for up to four hours ([News24](https://news24online.com/photos/entertainment/do-you-know-where-br-chopra-mahabharata-war-scenes-were-shot-and-how-lord-krishna-vishwaroop-was-filmed-832817)). That is the same as sai's "locked symmetrical frames for the sacred" rule. Avoid star-likeness and bad lip sync (the 2013 animated film). Avoid wall-to-wall effects and loud scoring (Star Plus). Respect iconography: *Adipurush* was attacked as a "Rs 500 crore Temple Run" with a "demonic" Ravana (reported, [Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/trending/disappointingadipurush-trends-as-netizens-call-it-rs-500-crore-temple-run-with-poor-vfx-demonic-depiction-of-ravana-437792/amp)). Check every frame for period errors (Ek Dharmayudh). Keep the scope finite, because even Star Plus's ₹100 crore of sets could not keep a 22-episode monthly schedule. Do not whitewash Karna. Ganguli's Krishna lists Draupadi's humiliation and Abhimanyu's killing, and that rebuke is the dramatic payoff of beat A17.

## AI-made series: AI worked only inside a human pipeline

**What worked.** Google's *Ancestra* gave the most detailed published hybrid recipe. The team scripted and storyboarded first and fine-tuned Imagen on reference images for consistency. Then it built "a virtual 3D model", recorded a draft camera move through it, and had Veo follow that motion. More than 200 traditional crew took part ([Google blog](https://blog.google/technology/google-deepmind/ancestra-behind-the-scenes/)). Amazon's *House of David* used AI as "puppetry": real actors and cameras, with AI worlds behind them. It went from about 70 AI images in season 1 to 350–400 in season 2 ([TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/house-of-david-season-2-showrunner-pro-ai-shots/)). Ek Dharmayudh was not prompt-only either. Actors in mocap suits drove it ([Deccan Herald/AFP](https://www.deccanherald.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/ai-is-rewiring-the-worlds-most-prolific-bollywood-industry-3955932)). It opened with more than 6.5 million views on day one ([Advanced Television](https://www.advanced-television.com/2025/10/30/india-record-breaking-launch-for-mahabharat-ek-dharamyudh)) and had at least 26.5 million views by about April 2026, by the company's count. Its cost claims conflict. The founder said about 1/100th of a ₹150–200 crore conventional cost. The head of Galleri5, which made it, later said one-fifth of the cost and a quarter of the time. The later, smaller figure is the safer one to plan with. China's AI comic-style dramas cut cost to RMB 1,000–2,500 per minute but still struggle with subtle faces ([Jiemian](https://en.jiemian.com/article/13650653.html)).

**What failed.** *Air Head* needed hundreds of generations for about 1.5 minutes of film, roughly 300 to 1. The balloon would not stay consistent and had to be rotoscoped and recoloured by hand ([fxguide](https://www.fxguide.com/?p=410279)). OpenAI shut Sora down in 2026, and the AI feature *Critterz* missed its Cannes premiere as a result ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/critterz-misses-cannes-openai-sora-shutdown)). That is a platform risk sai already faces with the Gemini pause. Ek Dharmayudh episode 1 put a "modern bedside table" and a picture of a man in a suit in Hastinapur ([India TV](https://www.indiatvnews.com/entertainment/ott/mahabharat-ek-dharmayudh-2025-ai-error-jiohotstar-2025-11-04-1015728)), and AFP reports an IMDb rating of 1.4/10. Aronofsky's AI series *On This Day… 1776* reportedly drew "harsh backlash" and the label "AI slop" ([Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/movies/darren-aronofskys-ai-generated-series-faces-harsh-backlash-online/)). sai's own Veo battle tests failed for the same reason. Text prompts do not give directable action.

**Hardware reality.** No source shows local AI video on a 4 GB card. FramePack and Wan2GP both put the floor at about 6 GB of VRAM ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/framepack-can-generate-ai-videos-locally-with-just-6gb-of-vram); [Wan2GP README](https://huggingface.co/vidfom/wan2gp/blob/main/README.md)). Any AI video for sai means a rented GPU or a paid API.

**The rules.** *YouTube disclosure:* only realistic AI content needs a label. Fully animated or fantastical content is exempt, and YouTube's own example is an "AI-generated… animation of a missile in a fully animated video". AI music that is the main focus does need disclosure. Cloning your own voice for dubs is exempt ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)). *Inauthentic content:* since July 2025, YouTube's monetisation rules target mass-produced and repetitive videos ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/09/youtube-prepares-crackdown-on-mass-produced-and-repetitive-videos-as-concern-over-ai-slop-grows)). A July 2026 clarification names "stitched-together AI clips with no logical arc" ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/13/youtube-inauthentic-content-monetization-policy-update/)). Original, hand-animated episodes sit clearly on the safe side. *Copyright:* the US Copyright Office says purely AI-generated material is not protected. The test is whether AI "is enhancing human expression or is the source of the expressive choices" ([Library of Congress](https://blogs.loc.gov/copyright/2025/05/a-i-art-and-copyright-the-human-element-that-makes-all-the-difference/)). India's position on who owns AI output is reported as unsettled ([Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/columns/indias-generative-ai-moment-copyright-law-at-regulatory-crossroads)). The Mahabharata text is public domain. The risks are copying the look of copyrighted serials, which sai's rule against feeding in film or TV frames already blocks, and failing the inauthentic-content test.

## Recommended pipeline: twelve steps built on the repo

Every step below starts from what already exists in studio/. "Claude" means the defined agents (showrunner, designer, storyboarder, reviewer, renderer, editor, publisher), driven by scripts. "sai" means sai's own hands and eyes. Costs are in US dollars. All are zero unless sai approves a paid step.

| # | Step | What to do | Tool | Cost | Who |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Canon lock | Re-check A01–A22 against Karna Parva lines 11579–12160. Write a one-page period-accuracy checklist (no modern props or furniture, correct banners and conch names). | Ganguli database, showrunner and reviewer agents | $0 | Claude drafts, sai signs off |
| 2 | Animatic lock | Add temp sound (the code conch, drone and hooves) to the HTML and Blender animatics. Fix every shot's length. Freeze the edit. | shots.json, layout.blend, cameras.json, ffmpeg | $0 | Claude builds, sai approves timing |
| 3 | Style bible and colour script | Paint 4–6 "Frank frames" over layout renders, plus a 6–8 frame colour script: dawn gold, then battle haze, then the darkening sun, then the cold aftermath. Write the rules: gold key, blue shadow, astra colours, outline weight. | Krita over Blender grabs | $0 | sai paints, Claude writes the bible |
| 4 | Shot grading | Grade every shot S/A/B/C (table below). Only S and A shots get bespoke 2D drawing or hand polish. | Spreadsheet field in shots.json | $0 | Claude proposes, sai approves |
| 5 | Asset repair and repaint | Fix hands, faces and finger skin stretch on the three hero rigs. Repaint albedo toward the painted target at 2K texture size. Add inverted-hull outlines. Make new props only if a shot needs them. | Blender 5.2 Texture Paint and Krita. New meshes: TRELLIS.2 on a rented 24 GB GPU, or the Meshy free tier (commercial use allowed with credit) | $0; TRELLIS rental about $0.69/hr on RunPod L4 | sai paints, Claude scripts fixes |
| 6 | Environments | Use painted mattes projected onto blockout geometry for far ground, Geometry Nodes armies for mid ground, and an Unreal plate where a camera needs real parallax. Light each Blender shot to its plate's sun direction. | Blender camera projection; Unreal 5.8 on sai's PC at low settings | $0 | Claude sets up, sai renders plates |
| 7 | Voice | Record the one optional line, the breaths and the exertion sounds against the locked animatic. Drive the visemes with Rhubarb. | sai's mic, Rhubarb, existing face rig | $0 | sai records, Claude syncs |
| 8 | Animation | Block on twos with anim_lib.py and moves.py. Put release frames on ones. Hold anticipation 4–8 frames. Polish only S and A shots by hand. Use phone video of sai's own bow draw as reference. | Blender, stepped interpolation | $0 | Claude blocks, sai polishes S and A shots |
| 9 | 2D FX library, then bespoke FX | Build a library once: arrow streak, gold sun-fire cycle, blue lightning set, shockwave ring, dust burst, sacred-geometry mandala, and two-tone impact-frame templates (gold/black for Karna, white-blue/black for Arjuna). Draw bespoke FX only on S shots. | Grease Pencil v3, existing Python/ffmpeg FX | $0 | Claude builds library, sai draws S-shot FX |
| 10 | Look and render | Render EEVEE in passes (character, FX, plate, light masks). Benchmark A06 on the GTX 1650 first. Keep cloud CPU Cycles for 960x540 drafts only. Send finals to SheepIt, or to a rented GPU if SheepIt stalls. | Blender EEVEE; SheepIt; RunPod or Vast | $0–$6 per episode (estimate below) | Claude renderer agent, sai approves the spend |
| 11 | Photography and grade | Add glow, light wrap, a small Kuwahara pass, paper grain and a slight colour offset at the frame edges. Match everything to the Frank frames. | Blender compositor, ffmpeg | $0 | Claude composites, sai reviews |
| 12 | Sound, mix, deliver, release | Use code-made beds plus Sonniss GDC sounds and CC0 Freesound files, with every licence logged. Mix to about −14 LUFS. Export 1080p H.264 (and an upscaled 4K) plus a 9:16 version from a second camera. Publish with a teaser animatic first. | numpy sound, ffmpeg, YouTube Studio | $0 | Claude editor and publisher agents, sai final approval |

A few step details carry evidence worth knowing. In step 5, TRELLIS.2 needs "at least 24GB" of GPU memory and is MIT-licensed ([TRELLIS.2 README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/microsoft/trellis.2@main/README.md)). Meshy's free models are CC BY 4.0, which "still allow[s] commercial use" if Meshy is credited ([Meshy help](https://intercom.help/meshy/en/articles/16102098-can-i-use-meshy-assets-commercially)). Tripo's free tier is reported as non-commercial ([CostBench](https://www.costbench.com/software/ai-3d-generation/tripo-ai/free-plan/)). Hunyuan3D 2.1 needs 29 GB in total, and its licence excludes the EU, UK and South Korea ([Hunyuan3D README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1/main/README.md)).

In step 8, the mocap free tiers are thin. Cascadeur's free plan is "Only non-commercial use" and cannot export FBX. Its Indie plan is $96 a year ([Cascadeur](https://cascadeur.com/plans)). Rokoko's free Starter allows 30 seconds of video processing a month ([Rokoko](https://rokoko.com/pricing/)). QuickMagic's free tier allows 30-second clips with FBX export, but its commercial terms are unverified ([iTechGuides](https://www.itechguides.com/products/quickmagic/pricing/)). For a 90-second duel that is mostly upper body, video reference plus hand keys beats paying for mocap.

In step 9, Grease Pencil v3 arrived in Blender 4.3, and its files do not open in 4.2 or earlier ([Blender docs](https://developer.blender.org/docs/release_notes/4.3/grease_pencil/)). Keep the PC, the cloud container and any farm on one Blender version.

In step 12, three YouTube details matter. Multi-language audio tracks need "Advanced features" access ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13338784?hl=en)). Shorts can run up to 3 minutes in square or vertical formats ([YouTube blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/tall-updates-coming-to-shorts/)). YouTube recommends 8 Mbps for 1080p SDR at 24–30 fps, H.264 High Profile and AAC at 48 kHz ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1722171?hl=en)). The −14 LUFS loudness target comes from third-party measurements, because YouTube does not publish it ([Production Advice](https://productionadvice.co.uk/youtube-loudness/)).

For the series, repeat steps 1–12 per episode, but steps 3, 5, 6 and 9 shrink each time. The style bible, rigs, sets and effects library carry over. That reuse is how *Flow*'s small team and *Super Giant Robot Brothers*' two-week episodes worked.

## Shot-by-shot effort grade for EP01

The first film's Fire Lotus scene was "barely ranking at level B" ([Shenzhen Daily](http://www.szdaily.com/content/2025-03/03/content_31477678.htm)). Here, S means bespoke hand-drawn FX plus hand polish. A means full 3D animation with library FX. B means 3D on twos with simple or no FX. C means a held or near-held frame with camera moves, paint and compositing. Shot numbers and beats follow `studio/episodes/ep01_arjuna_vs_karna/shots.md`.

## Conclusion

The research changes the question sai should ask. It is no longer "which pipeline should I copy?" Every successful small production (*Flow*, *Schirkoa*, *Hanu-Man*, *Legend of Hanuman*) already sits in the family sai has built. The question is where to spend the hours. *Ne Zha 2* had 4,000 animators and still graded shots by difficulty. A solo creator gains even more from it. Five S shots and a shared effects library make a 90-second duel finishable in weeks rather than months. The other surprise is that render cost is close to solved. A free farm or a $3 GPU night replaces the slow CPU path. The real bottleneck is sai's review time and drawing time, so the plan moves as much of steps 1–12 as possible to scripts and agents and keeps sai's hands on the frames that decide the episode.

The competitive gap is also clearer than expected. Netflix has the scale and an anime style. The AI series has the reach and a 1.4 IMDb rating. Nobody owns a faithful, painterly, short-form Mahabharata that stages Ganguli's beats exactly: the serpent arrow, the chariot pressed into the earth, the Anjalika in light rather than blood. That is a narrow lane, but it is open, and EP01 as designed sits in it.
