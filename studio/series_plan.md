# Series plan: the Mahabharat in story order

Status: **draft for sai's review** (2026-11-26).

## Decisions so far (sai, 2026-11-26)

- **Order:** the series is told in story order. It runs from Vyasa dictating the epic to Ganesha until Gandhari's curse on Krishna after the war.
- **Opening:** the series opens with Vyasa, not with Sauti's frame story.
- **Side stories:** Nala and Damayanti, Savitri, and Bhima meeting Hanuman are included. All three are in the Ganguli Vana Parva, at the chapters listed below. Shakuntala is added as well, because she starts the Bharata line.
- **Length:** episodes run about **20 minutes**.
- **The duel:** the finished Arjuna vs Karna duel ("The Anjalika") is released first as the **teaser**. It returns inside episode 21.

This closes the open question in `preproduction/COMMS/VISION_PLAN.md` (item 10, "Kurukshetra or Adi Parva").

## How a 20-minute episode is built

- **Scenes:** each episode is about 13 to 15 scenes of 60 to 90 seconds, made with the twelve-step pipeline in `pipeline.md`.
- **Key scenes:** the key scenes listed below are the anchors of each episode. The connecting scenes come from the same chapters during that episode's canon lock.
- **Release:** each scene is released as a Short or Reel as soon as it is finished. The full 20-minute episode follows when all its scenes are done, so the channel keeps posting while long episodes are built.
- **Scale:** the 90-second teaser has 22 shots, so a 20-minute episode is roughly 250 to 300 shots. That is about 13 times the teaser, which is why scenes ship one by one.
- **Chapter ids:** ids such as `B01C100` point to `mahabharat-research/db/chapters.csv`, which gives each id's line range in `source/`. Ranges marked "about" are confirmed during that episode's canon lock.

## What this changes in the repo

- The duel folder keeps its name, `episodes/ep01_arjuna_vs_karna/`, so that no links break. In new documents it is called **the teaser**, and in the series it becomes part of **episode 21**.
- New work goes in folders named `episodes/s<season>e<ep>_<slug>/scNN/`, one folder per scene. For example, `episodes/s1e01_the_scribe/sc01/` is Vyasa and Ganesha.
