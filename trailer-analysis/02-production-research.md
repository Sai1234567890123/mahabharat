# How Baahubali: The Eternal War is being made

Research summary from public press coverage up to October 2026. Facts are cited; anything marked **inferred** is our reading of the frames plus the known methods of the artists and studios involved, not something the production has confirmed. The production has not published its software list or a technical breakdown.

## 1. The project

- Two-part animated feature that continues S.S. Rajamouli's Baahubali universe. Part 1, *The Eternal War*, is set for a 2027 release. ([Cartoon Brew](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/feature-film/baahubali-the-eternal-war-animated-feature-trailer-256369.html), [AWN](https://www.awn.com/news/teaser-drops-baahubali-eternal-war-part-1-animated-feature))
- Story: after Amarendra Baahubali is betrayed and killed, he travels through the afterlife and is drawn into a cosmic war between devas and asuras across the 14 realms. ([80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/this-animated-project-from-india-involving-ex-arcane-artists-looks-insane), [AWN](https://www.awn.com/news/teaser-drops-baahubali-eternal-war-part-1-animated-feature))
- The teaser premiered in theaters with the re-release *Baahubali: The Epic* and went online on 4 November 2025. Reviewers singled out the jump from live action into animation. ([Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/art/2d-animation/baahubali-the-eternal-war-trailer-stuns-arcane-and-spider-verse-fans))
- Work-in-progress footage was shown at the Annecy festival on 25 June 2026. ([Cartoon Brew, Annecy](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/feature-film/baahubali-the-eternal-war-rajamouli-annecy-263875.html), [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-news/studio-khara-joins-baahubali-the-eternal-war-part-1-production))

## 3. Stated creative intent

From the Annecy presentation ([Cartoon Brew, Annecy](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/feature-film/baahubali-the-eternal-war-rajamouli-annecy-263875.html)):

- Shukla wanted a painterly look that matches the concept art, neither old-fashioned animation nor hyper-realism. He said it is tragic when the final CG looks nothing like the concept art.
- Sakuga-style anime influence was added because the film is about gods fighting.
- Characters are deliberately larger than life ("almost like superhumans, so we decided to double down on that").
- Stylizing the hero while keeping him recognizable as the actor took many iterations; the hair was the hardest part.
- The film is primarily CG, with one 2D sequence by Studio Khara.
- The mythology research took the writers far longer than expected.
- The world includes the 14 realms, giant eagles, double-headed elephants and vimanas (flying palaces).
- Action uses Kalaripayattu; dance draws on Chhau and Kuchipudi.

## 4. The pipeline, stage by stage

The stages below follow standard feature-animation practice and the roles the press assigns to each studio. Tool names are **inferred** unless a source is given.

1. **Development and mythology research.** Writers build the story and the cosmology of the 14 realms. (Confirmed: Mosier and Sharma; research was long.)
2. **Pre-production package.** Aniventure, Zaratan and Alcyde produce a pitch package; Alcyde develops the look. (Confirmed, AWN.)
3. **Visual development.** Concept paintings, color scripts, character turnarounds, the hero's stylized likeness. (Confirmed: Alcyde leads. Tools **inferred**: Photoshop or Procreate for paintings; ZBrush for sculpts.)
4. **Storyboards and animatic.** (Confirmed: Alcyde. Tools **inferred**: Storyboard Pro or similar, edited into an animatic.)
5. **3D previs and layout.** Rough 3D sets and cameras define every shot's staging and lens. (Confirmed that Alcyde does 3D previs. **Inferred**: Shukla's own earlier film used Unreal Engine for layout and real-time previs, see section 5.)
6. **Modeling, texturing, rigging.** Characters and sets built in 3D with painted textures. (**Inferred** from the frames: hand-painted texture maps, simplified hair clumps, stylized proportions.)
7. **Character animation.** Keyframed performance plus action choreography; likely motion capture or video reference for dance and martial art. (Confirmed: 88 Pictures leads animation. Mocap use **inferred**.)
8. **2D effects animation.** Fire, lightning, energy, smoke and impact frames drawn by hand or in a 2D tool, often on twos. (**Inferred** from the frames.)
9. **Lighting and stylized rendering.** Non-photoreal shading with painted light and colored rim light. (**Inferred**.)
10. **Compositing.** 2D FX, painted skies, line-art mandalas and lens effects combined; grade per sequence. (**Inferred**: Nuke and After Effects are the industry standard for this.)
11. **Editorial, sound, music, voice.** Original cast records in multiple languages (the teaser exists in Telugu and Hindi). ([YouTube, Hindi teaser](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GJKsRRX3v1Q))

## 5. Methods from the people involved

### Ishan Shukla's real-time workflow (on *Schirkoa*)

From an interview with [befores & afters](https://beforesandafters.com/2020/08/31/schirkoa-director-ishan-shukla-on-moving-to-a-real-time-workflow/):

- Characters built in Reallusion Character Creator, with Daz for special parts such as costumes and horns.
- Motion capture with Perception Neuron suits and iClone, plus phone-based face capture.
- Hero characters and key motion polished in Maya or Blender, exported as FBX or Alembic.
- Sets, layout, lighting and the 3D animatic in Unreal Engine, iterated with cuts in Premiere Pro.
- Stylized look from post-process materials and color correction inside Unreal; for the earlier short, extra toon render passes for outlines and diffuse, merged in After Effects; hand-painted textures filtered flat.
- The original short was made on a single workstation.

This shows the director is comfortable with real-time engines, mocap and small-team pipelines, which is relevant for us.

### The *Arcane* method (Fortiche), via Alcyde's ex-Arcane artists

From [RedShark News](https://redsharknews.com/why-netflixs-arcane-looks-so-good-how-fortiche-ramped-up-the-animation-pipeline):

- Maya for modeling, rigging, animation and virtual cameras; Photoshop for texture painting and hand-drawn FX; Nuke as the main compositor; After Effects for hand-drawn FX and motion graphics.
- Hand-painted textures and painted light and shadow on 3D assets instead of procedural shading.
- 2D effects animated at 12 fps while characters and environments run at 24 fps.
- 2D matte paintings projected onto simple 3D geometry for parallax.
- Departments worked in parallel layers; the team grew from 5 people to over 300.

### The *Spider-Verse* influence

Press compares the teaser to *Spider-Verse*. The visible shared techniques are: impact frames, drawn speed lines and smears over 3D, chromatic aberration as a stylistic choice, and switching rendering styles inside one shot. ([Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/art/2d-animation/baahubali-the-eternal-war-trailer-stuns-arcane-and-spider-verse-fans), [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/articles/baahubali-eternal-wars-arcane-inspired-083000727.html))

## 6. What this means for a small team

- The look comes from **art direction discipline**, not from one magic tool: painted textures, a strict color script, drawn FX and graphic editing.
- The big studios split the work across eight companies and hundreds of artists. A small team cannot match that frame for frame; it has to replace labor with **AI generation for paintings and in-between work**, **open-source 3D for control**, and **automation (Claude Code) for the glue**.
- The hardest problems they reported (matching concept art, stylized likeness, hair) are exactly the problems AI image tools are good at, as long as a 3D layout controls composition and consistency.
