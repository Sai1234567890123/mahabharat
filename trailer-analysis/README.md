# Trailer analysis

Reference research for the Mahabharat AI series, based on the *Baahubali: The Eternal War* teaser.

1. [01-shot-analysis.md](01-shot-analysis.md): act structure, an 82-row shot list with timing, camera language, transitions, how 2D and 3D are combined, lighting and color, world-building, and ten style rules for our series.
2. [02-production-research.md](02-production-research.md): who is making the film, their stated intent, the pipeline stage by stage, and the methods of the director and the ex-Arcane team, with sources.
3. [03-build-plan.md](03-build-plan.md): how we build the same quality with Blender and other open-source tools, Google Cloud (Vertex AI, Veo, Gemini, GPU VMs), AI image and video models, and Claude Code.

All shots are described in words. No trailer frames are copied or embedded, so these documents stay valid after the screenshots are deleted.
